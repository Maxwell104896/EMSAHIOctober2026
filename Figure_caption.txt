Supplementary Figure S4. Training-only model tuning, calibration and next-visit HI forecast comparisons.
All panels use the same 152 ordered windows from 66 participants and the locked outer-participant folds and HI axes used in finalized Figures 1–3. No actual next-visit interval is a predictor. (A) Inner-cross-validation MAE for 13 Ridge penalties across six feature families, averaged across five outer-training sets. Each outer-training set uses four participant-grouped inner splits. One- and two-visit families include HI(t2) or HI(t1)/HI(t2), respectively. Dynamic features are none, prior change, or prior change plus absolute change and velocity. All families include the previously observed interval dt12 in weeks. Change is HI(t2)−HI(t1) and velocity is change/dt12. Features containing both scores and their difference are algebraically redundant. These correlated configuration comparisons are training diagnostics, not independent validations. Selected fold configurations are supplied in the accompanying table. (B) Calibration of the locked model's outer-fold predictions in five approximately equal-size prediction quantile bins. Numbers indicate windows per bin. Vertical bars give percentile 95% intervals from 2,000 participant-cluster bootstrap replicates with bins fixed from the original OOF predictions. Calibration intercept and slope describe observed HI regressed on predicted HI and do not modify the predictions. (C) Observed-minus-predicted residuals of the locked model. (D–I) Outer-fold next-visit predictions for persistence, prior-change continuation, locked Ridge, Ridge with four MBX modules, and the corresponding shrinkage versions. All scatter panels use the same axis limits and molecular state colors. Persistence predicts HI(t2). Prior-change continuation predicts HI(t2)+[HI(t2)−HI(t1)] without time extrapolation. The locked Ridge reproduces the finalized predictions exactly. The module model adds bile acid, oxidative lipid, aromatic amino acid/indole and SCFA/butyrate module scores at t2; Ridge alpha is selected only within the outer-training windows. Module inputs are inner-crossfit for outer-training samples and outer-heldout for test samples, with upstream module C inherited from the finalized outer-training module layer. These are exploratory conditional comparisons on the locked HI axes and upstream hyperparameter choices, rather than a newly optimized end-to-end external validation. Shrinkage multiplies the predicted HI change by lambda in [0,1], estimated by least squares from inner predictions in each outer-training set. Lambda equals 1 in all five folds for both models, so H equals F and I equals G. Model scores and shrinkage coefficients are supplied for audit. MAE confidence intervals and paired model differences resample participants with all their windows together. Intervals condition on the locked fitted models and do not incorporate retraining variability. Colors denote observed t3 Low, Intermediate and High molecular HI states, using lower and upper training-tertile thresholds of the corresponding outer fold; counts are 55, 50 and 47. These molecular states are not clinical relapse endpoints.
